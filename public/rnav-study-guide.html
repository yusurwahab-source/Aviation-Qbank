📡 Properties of Radio Waves

The physics every radio aid rests on: what a radio wave is, how it is polarized, how frequency and wavelength convert, which band each aid uses, and how phase is compared.

📡 Nature of Radio Waves 6 questions

What a radio wave physically is.

💡 Key idea

High-frequency alternating current makes a wire radiate. Direct current gives a steady magnetic field around the wire. As the frequency rises, the field has not finished collapsing when the reversed field starts to build, and energy leaves the wire as electromagnetic radiation. The electric (E) and magnetic (H) fields are at right angles to each other. Speed: 300,000,000 m/s, about 162,000 NM per second.

🧭 Polarization 9 questions

Which way the electric field points.

💡 Key idea

Polarization is the plane of the electric field. A vertical aerial emits a vertically polarized wave and a horizontal aerial a horizontally polarized one. For maximum signal the receiving aerial must lie in the same plane. Circular polarization (e.g. from a helical antenna) rotates the field at the wave frequency: a dipole's orientation then no longer matters, and radar can remove precipitation clutter because reflection from water droplets reverses the circularity.

⏱️ Period and Frequency 8 questions

Time for one cycle, and its reciprocal.

💡 Key idea

f = 1/τ. Period is the time for one cycle (microseconds). 1 kHz = 10³ Hz, 1 MHz = 10⁶ Hz, 1 GHz = 10⁹ Hz. Example: τ = 0.125 µs gives f = 1/(0.125×10⁻⁶) = 8 MHz.

📏 Speed and Wavelength 14 questions

Turning frequency into distance.

💡 Key idea

λ = c/f = c × τ. With c = 300×10⁶ m/s: f (MHz) = 300 / λ (m) and λ (m) = 300 / f (MHz). Examples: 121.5 MHz → 2.47 m; 375 kHz = 0.375 MHz → 800 m; 3.2 cm = 0.032 m → 9375 MHz = 9.375 GHz. Always convert units first.

🔄 Phase Comparison 8 questions

The principle behind VOR-type systems.

💡 Key idea

Same frequency, one reference. Compare a variable wave with a reference wave of the same frequency. Phase difference = reference − variable; if negative, add 360°. Example: 110° − 315° = −205° → 155°.
